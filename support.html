How it works

Set a start point (your location, an address or a tap on the map), choose a distance, what you like (parks, waterfront, quiet streets) and terrain, and RunTheTown draws three loops that keep to promenades, park paths and quiet streets wherever the map allows. Tap a route to see it, tap again to start voice guidance. "Run in laps" plans laps at a nearby athletics track or park, with the way there and back.

Common questions

  • The voice stops when I lock the phone. On the website version this is a limit of web pages on iPhone; the App Store version keeps guiding with the phone locked.
  • Zoe and Tom are not in the voice list. The website version can only use the phone's built-in voices; the App Store version offers every voice installed on the phone.
  • "No runnable paths found near the start point". Move the pin onto a street or path. In villages and open country the app includes unpaved lanes and tracks automatically.
  • The map pieces didn't load. Check the connection and press "Find my routes" again; pieces already downloaded are kept.
  • Where are my runs? Menu → My runs. They live on your device; Export saves them all as a file.

Updating

The App Store version updates through the App Store. The website version updates itself on the next open; the version number is shown at the bottom of the menu.
