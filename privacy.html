RunTheTown privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Back to the app · Support

The short version

RunTheTown has no server and no account. Everything you type or record stays on your device. The app talks to public map services only to fetch the map around the place you are planning a run.

What stays on your device

  • Your profile (name, e-mail address, photo), kept only so the app can show it to you and put your name on a run picture you choose to share. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your runs (date, distance, time, pace, the planned route, the GPS track and a picture of the route), kept in the app's own storage on the device. You can delete any run, and you can export them all as a file.
  • Your settings (voice, units, announcements) and the cached map data for places you planned runs in, kept up to 14 days so they load faster.

Deleting the app removes all of this. On the website version, clearing the site data in your browser does the same.

Location

RunTheTown uses your location to set the start point, to find running spots around you, and, during a run, to follow you along the route so it can read the turns and count your distance. In the iPhone app this continues while the phone is locked or in your pocket, which is why the app asks for location access "while using" and runs in the background during a run only. Your location is never sent to a RunTheTown server (there is none) and never stored by us.

To draw the map and plan a route, the app sends the coordinates of the area you are looking at to the public services listed below. They receive an area, not your identity, and each has its own privacy policy.

Services the app uses

  • OpenFreeMap for streets, paths and parks; Esri and OpenStreetMap for the background map pictures.
  • Microsoft Planetary Computer (ESA WorldCover) for land cover, and AWS Terrain Tiles / Open-Meteo for elevation.
  • Overpass API for traffic lights and crossings.
  • Photon (komoot) and Nominatim for address search and for finding tracks, stadiums and parks; Wikidata and Wikipedia for a place's website and article.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking across apps or sites, no selling or sharing of data. There are no third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics in the app.

Reporting a problem

"Report a problem" in the app opens your own e-mail app with a message to us about a route (the reason you picked, your note, the route name and the position you reported from). It is only sent if you press send in your e-mail app.

Children

RunTheTown is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
